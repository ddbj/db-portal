## About this policy

This policy sets out the terms governing the websites, information pages, contact features, submission and search assistance features, and LLM-powered features operated by the Bioinformation and DDBJ Center (hereinafter "BSI").

This policy is established so that BSI can deliver integrated information from DDBJ and DBCLS in a coordinated way.

## Relationship to existing DDBJ and DBCLS policies

When using the BSI websites and related services, the existing terms of use, site policies, privacy policies, and individual database and service terms maintained by DDBJ and DBCLS continue to apply in addition to this policy.

- For databases, submission, search, and analysis services provided by DDBJ, the terms, guidelines, and conditions of use of each DDBJ service apply.
- For websites, databases, tools, and information services provided by DBCLS, the DBCLS site policy and the terms of each service apply.
- For JGA, AGD, the NBDC Human Database, and other controlled-access or human-subject data services, the applicable data sharing guidelines, application conditions, consent terms, and access control rules apply.

When this policy differs from the terms of an individual service, the terms of that individual service take precedence. However, for LLM-powered features, the terms set out in this policy apply additionally.

Users should treat the information on this site as a reference. For important decisions, please consult the official documentation, the terms of each service, the relevant guidelines, and the responsible contact desks.

This site may link to external sites, services, or databases. Use of those external resources is governed by their own terms and policies.

## Use of data and content

Data, documents, figures, software, APIs, metadata, and other content provided through this site must be used in accordance with the licence, terms of use, database terms, or data provider conditions explicitly attached to each item.

In particular, human-subject data, controlled-access data, application-required data, and consent-based data must be used in accordance with the applicable guidelines, approved conditions, and terms of use.

## Handling of personal information and logs

BSI may collect and retain the following information to the extent necessary for operating the site, handling enquiries, improving services, responding to incidents, ensuring security, and preventing misuse:

- IP address
- Access date and time
- Accessed pages
- Browser, OS, and other environment information
- Contents of enquiries
- Questions, instructions, search conditions, and feedback submitted to LLM-powered features
- Responses, generated queries, and search assistance logs produced by LLM-powered features

The information collected is used for service operation, responding to enquiries, quality improvement, incident investigation, security response, prevention of misuse, and statistical analysis of usage.

Logs are retained for one year from collection as a general rule. They may, however, be retained beyond one year as necessary for incident investigation, security response, audit, or legal compliance.

Logs and submitted information may be reviewed by BSI, DDBJ, and DBCLS operations staff, system administrators, developers, enquiry handlers, and contractors as needed for their duties.

## About the LLM-powered features

To help users understand and use information, databases, submission procedures, and documentation related to BSI, DDBJ, and DBCLS, BSI may offer features powered by large language models (LLMs) and other AI technologies.

LLM-powered features may include:

- Chat
- Query builder
- Document search assistance
- Summarisation
- Classification
- Guidance
- Text generation
- Other similar functions

The output of LLM-powered features may be generated automatically based on user input, public information, reference materials prepared by BSI, search results, and documentation.

The output does not constitute an official position, a final judgement, a review outcome, an approval, a determination on data submission eligibility, a determination on data use authorisation, legal advice, or medical advice.

For important decisions, please always confirm the official documentation, the terms of each service, the relevant guidelines, and the responsible contact desks.

## LLM execution environment

The LLMs used by these features run within an environment managed by BSI as local LLMs.

The questions, instructions, search conditions, query-generation inputs, feedback, and other inputs submitted by users to LLM-powered features are not transmitted to external commercial LLM APIs, external AI services, or external cloud AI platforms for the purposes of response generation, query generation, search assistance, summarisation, classification, or guidance.

Processing of LLM-powered features is performed, as a general rule, using servers, networks, storage, search indexes, and knowledge bases under BSI's control.

Input submitted to LLM-powered features is not used for general-purpose LLM training. However, logs may be analysed under BSI's control for evaluating response quality, improving search and query-generation accuracy, improving the UI, investigating incidents, performing security checks, and preventing misuse.

## Notes on input to LLM-powered features

LLM-powered features run in a fully local environment, and input is not transmitted to external commercial LLM APIs or similar services.

However, the input, the generated queries, the responses, the search-assistance flow, the access date and time, the IP address, the environment information, and the feedback are stored as logs, and staff may review them as needed for their duties.

For this reason, users must not submit the following information to LLM-powered features, except when BSI explicitly provides a dedicated feature that requests such input:

- Authentication credentials such as passwords, API keys, access tokens, and private keys
- Information that can directly identify an individual
- Individual-level human-subject data
- Controlled-access data, application-required data, and unpublished data
- Information confidential under contract or research ethics
- Information whose sharing, input, or analysis is restricted by consent conditions, terms of use, or guidelines
- Information that infringes the rights of third parties
- Information that violates laws, public order, or research ethics

## Prohibited uses

Users must not use this site or the LLM-powered features for any of the following purposes:

- Unauthorised access, bypassing authentication, exploiting vulnerabilities, or interfering with the system
- Inappropriate input, acquisition, sharing, or leakage of personal information, confidential information, or controlled-access data
- Use of human-subject data or controlled-access data outside the scope of approval
- Acts that infringe the rights of third parties
- Generating or distributing false information, misinformation, discriminatory expressions, defamation, or harmful content
- Acts that violate laws, research ethics, or the terms of use of any database
- Acts that improperly circumvent or interfere with the restrictions, protections, or logging of LLM-powered features

When BSI confirms an act that falls within, or could fall within, the prohibited uses, BSI may take measures such as suspending use, reviewing logs, contacting the relevant organisations, or any other action it deems necessary.
