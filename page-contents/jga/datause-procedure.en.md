## Detailed Steps for the Data Use Application

**Creating a Data Use Group:**

  - After logging in to the [application system](https://humandbs.ddbj.nig.ac.jp/nbdc/application), select [Create Group] from the menu on the left side of the screen
  - Enter the group name, group description, and group type "Data Use Group", then click the [Save] button
  - Add members to the created Data Use Group
    - Click the [Invite Member] button on the [Members] tab
    - Enter the **institutional email address** or the **DDBJ account** of the principal investigator, the applicant, and the data download contact
    - Clicking the [Invite] button sends an email to the invited member.
    - Once the member accepts the invitation as instructed in the email, they are registered as a member of the group.
  - Grant PI privileges to the principal investigator for the Data Use application
    - This can be changed by a member who has been granted Owner privileges
    - If the principal investigator for the Data Use application created the group, no change is needed, since that person already has PI/Owner status
    - Select the radio button next to PI for the relevant member, then click [Change Owner/PI]

**Updating DDBJ Account Information:**

  - Your full name, institution, department, job title, and phone number in Japanese, and your address, institution, department, and job title in English, are required
    - Because applicant information is carried over from your DDBJ account into the system automatically, you must add this information to your registered DDBJ account details
  - After logging in to the [application system](https://humandbs.ddbj.nig.ac.jp/nbdc/application), click [DDBJ Account] under "Application List" at the top right of the screen
  - After updating the information, click [Update Profile]

**Data Use Application:**

  - After logging in to the [application system](https://humandbs.ddbj.nig.ac.jp/nbdc/application), click [Submit a Data Use Application]
  - Select either "Application from within Japan" or "Application from outside Japan", then click [Start a New Application]
  - Fill in the required information on the input form and attach the necessary documents
    - Select the Data Use Group
    - If you need to change the principal investigator, update the registered information of the Data Use Group
    - Enter information about co-investigators who are not members of the Data Use Group under "Other Co-investigators"
    - The head of institution is the person who authorizes the execution of a research plan approved by the ethics review board; enter the information of the person currently holding the position designated as "head of the research institution" in the review outcome notice or an equivalent document
    - For the research title, enter the title exactly as it appears in the research plan that received both ethics review board approval and head-of-institution approval to proceed
    - When attaching documents such as the research plan, the implementation approval document, and the NBDC Human Data Security Guidelines checklist, specify at least one file type
  - On the "Overview" page, check that the entered information is correct, then click the [Apply] button
