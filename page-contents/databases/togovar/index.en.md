## About TogoVar

TogoVar is a database that aggregates and integrates published human genome variants.
You can submit variants referenced against GRCh37 or GRCh38, annotated with frequency and functional information. DDBJ's submission navigator directs public human variant data to TogoVar.

> [!NOTE]
> Choose human × variant in the [submission navigator](/submit) to see the TogoVar registration path.
